How we work

First we talk about your dreams

Six steps from the first meeting to the day your team moves in.

  1. Brief

    We listen: how your team works, how it will grow, budget and timeline.

  2. Concept

    Layout options, mood and materials – global influences, local design.

  3. Detailed design

    Detailed drawings, specifications and a schedule of works, coordinated with all consultants.

  4. VR walkthrough

    Walk through your new space in virtual reality and adjust it before anything is built.

  5. Build

    Our project managers supervise the works on site, on schedule and on budget.

  6. Move in

    Enjoy your new space – and we keep checking in after you move in.
